A Church Where You Belong

The Covenant of Light Church is a family of believers passionate about knowing God, growing in faith, and making a difference in our community.

We believe church is more than a building. It is a place where people can encounter God, build meaningful relationships, discover their purpose, and experience the joy of walking together in faith.

THE CONVENER

Pastor David Teminijesu

Welcome to Covenant of Light Church. Our convener, Pastor David Teminijesu, is passionate about building a Christ-centered community where people can grow in faith, discover purpose, and serve others.

Through dedicated leadership and a heart for people, he continues to help shape the vision of Covenant of Light and create a place where everyone can encounter God and find a sense of belonging.
